You have probably already bought the tools you need. The problem is that they do not talk to each other, so people become the integration — copying data across, reconciling what each system thinks is true. This service connects those systems and then reports on where work actually slows down, so improvements are aimed at the real bottleneck.

## The symptoms it addresses

- The same record lives in several systems, and none of them agree.
- You pay for tools whose data never reaches the place a decision is made.
- You cannot tell which step in a process is the one costing you time.

## What we deliver

- Integrations between the systems you already run, so data moves without a person carrying it.
- A single, current view of the records that matter, drawn from the systems of record.
- Process reporting that shows where work waits, how long, and how often.

## Where a human stays in the loop

Integrations move data; they do not overwrite judgment. Where two systems disagree, the conflict is surfaced for a person to resolve. The reporting points to the bottleneck — deciding what to do about it stays with you.
