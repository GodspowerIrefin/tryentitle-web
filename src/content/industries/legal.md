A law practice generates a document for nearly every action it takes. Most of that work is not legal judgment it is intake, formatting, filing, and chasing signatures. We take on the repeatable document handling so your attorneys and paralegals spend their time on the matter, not the paperwork trail behind it.

## Workflows we take on

- **Matter intake and conflict checks** — capturing a new matter's details once and running the conflict check before the file is opened.
- **Engagement letters** — generating the letter from the matter details and tracking it through signature.
- **Discovery document handling** — organizing, indexing, and de-duplicating productions so review starts from a clean set.
- **Court and filing deadlines** — pulling dates from orders and filings into your calendar so nothing depends on someone remembering.
- **Inbound inquiries** — responding to new leads on arrival, capturing the details, and booking the consult before the caller moves on.

## Where a human stays in the loop

Legal judgment stays with your attorneys. We assemble, organize, and route; an attorney reviews anything substantive before it goes out, and any conflict or missing detail is surfaced for a person rather than assumed away.
